The Origin

The origin is personal. The why is universal.

This conviction did not come from a textbook. It came from lived experience.

Our Chairperson, Bassel Hamwi, built Bank Audi Syria from a startup into the country’s largest private lender, helped start its first stock market, and then led the institution through civil war: bank runs, bombings, sanctions, and the collapse of the correspondent banking system, making decisions where the wrong call could endanger lives, not just balance sheets. Over those years, most of what he had built fell apart. Career, finances, sense of identity.

What stayed with him was not the loss. It was the loneliness of it. There was no one to think with. No one to help him see clearly when everything was unclear. He eventually rebuilt. He never forgot what it felt like to face the hardest chapter without a thinking partner.

EthosQuest grew out of that experience. Not as a monument to resilience, but as a practical answer to a simple question: what if no leader had to go through their hardest moments without someone in their corner?

The hardest thing about leadership is not the decision. It is facing the decision alone.

Our Values

Five commitments. No exceptions.

Transparency

We say what we see. The coaching relationship is built on honesty, not comfort.

Precision

We do not deal in vague inspiration. We are specific about what we observe, what we ask, and what we recommend.

Clarity

We help leaders cut through noise, emotion, and complexity to see what is actually true.

Impact

The work must produce real change in how leaders think, decide, and live. If it does not, we say so.

Curiosity

We approach every leader with genuine interest in who they are, what they carry, and what they are solving for. We never assume we already know.

The Coach’s Stance

Not a neutral facilitator.

The coach does not have an opinion about what you should do. The coach has a commitment to ensuring you see clearly, decide honestly, and act in alignment with what you actually value. When the coach mirrors back, you are not hearing their perspective. You are hearing your own truth, organized and reflected with precision.

Meet the CoachesMeet the Coaches

Completely in your camp

The client comes first, always. Not detached facilitation. Not neutral observation. The coach adopts your values, fights for your interests, and holds your life and choices at the center of every conversation.

Maximally available during takeoff

If a client needs ten meetings a month plus hours-long calls in the first ninety days, the answer is yes.

Does their own work

The coach takes the same assessments the client takes, and faces their own dragons first. This is not about appearing knowledgeable. It is about understanding from the inside what the client is experiencing.

Shows up at full capacity

Every session requires the coach to be fully present, fully clear, with the energy and focus to read the client in real time.

Adopts your values, not their own

The methodology is neutral on religious, spiritual, and philosophical belief systems. The coach works within your frame of meaning, not above it or outside it.

The Discovery Call

The first act of the work.

The discovery call is not a sales conversation. The coach listens, mirrors back what was heard, explains the structure, and you decide. The coach does not sell. The coach demonstrates.
