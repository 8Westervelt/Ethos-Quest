Background and Perspective

The experience behind the conversation.

Bassel brings a financial markets and operating leadership perspective to executive coaching. His career spans banking, investment, and private equity, with a particular interest in governance, family business, and supporting senior leaders.

He founded and led Bank Audi Syria from 2005 to 2014, headed the IFC MENA Fund from 2014 to 2022, and served on the YPO Global Board from 2018 to 2022. These experiences inform his understanding of organizational responsibility and leadership under pressure.
