The Pressures

What this seat carries.

Success that stops answering the question

The milestones have been reached. What they were meant to give you is less clear than it used to be.

An identity tied to what you built

For years, a company or a portfolio answered the question of who you are. After a sale or a step back, it no longer does.

Decisions with no second chance

Selling, stepping back, or starting again: choices about timing, people, and capital that are made once.

Few people to think with

Advisers, partners, and family all have a stake in what you decide. Very few have none.

The Work

How coaching helps.

There is no set program. Every coaching partnership starts with where you are and the questions advice alone cannot answer.

Reinvention

Working out what you want the next chapter to be, rather than defaulting to more of the last one.

Purpose

Clarity on what genuinely matters to you now, and on where your time, energy, and judgment are best spent.

Legacy

Deciding what you want your work, your capital, and your name to stand for, including what you hand on to your family.

Fulfillment

Attention to the parts of life that achievement has crowded out, including relationships, well-being, and joy.

Questions Leaders Ask

In your own words.

I’m thinking about selling the business I built. Would coaching help?

Yes. The work holds the practical decisions about the sale alongside the personal ones, so you sign knowing what you want and stay steady through what comes after.

I sold my company six months ago and feel lost. Is that normal?

It is very common, and rarely talked about. Coaching gives you space to understand what you have lost and to decide what you want to build next.

I’ve achieved what I set out to, but it doesn’t feel the way I expected. Can coaching help?

Yes. That gap is often where the most useful work begins: looking honestly at what you value now, and at what a fulfilling next chapter would actually involve.
