What this seat carries.

Everything at once

Questions Leaders Ask

In your own words.

My company has tripled in two years and I’m busier than ever but less effective. Would coaching help?

Very often, yes. The work looks at where your attention is actually going and why, then helps you build the focus the next stage requires.

I’m a founder and my board is talking about bringing in an experienced CEO. How should I think about it?

Start with what you want, separately from what you fear. Coaching gives you room to look at the decision honestly before it is made for you.

My co-founder and I no longer agree on where the company is going. Can coaching help?

Coaching works with you, not with the partnership as a whole. It can help you get clear on what you want, what you are willing to change, and how to have the conversation you have been avoiding.
